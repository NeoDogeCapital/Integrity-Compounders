Method & caveats

Return series is reconstructed from target-allocation snapshots × daily adjusted prices, rebalanced at each snapshot; validated against the original engine (median daily difference 0.00000%). Inception is 2025-10-10 — the 2019-09-13 allocation row is a Koyfin reference, not a portfolio state. With ~9 months of history, annualised figures (CAGR, Calmar, Treynor) are directionally indicative only. Up/down capture is geometric on daily observations (the monthly convention would give ~9 data points). Batting average and slugging are trade-level across YTD allocation episodes (entry→exit, open positions marked to last close) — distinct from the monthly win rate shown in Risk & ratios.
